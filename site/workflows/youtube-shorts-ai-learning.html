Open workflow sourceReview quality gate

Production loop

Scene-first, evidence-first video creation.

  1. Research official sources and write a fact-checked digest.
  2. Storyboard 6–8 shots before generating visuals.
  3. Generate cinematic key visuals with no embedded text.
  4. Generate one Supertonic narration file per scene.
  5. Render image-based motion with large burned-in captions.
  6. Inspect preview contact sheets and fix clipping or pseudo-text.
  7. Upload only after quality gate and approval.
